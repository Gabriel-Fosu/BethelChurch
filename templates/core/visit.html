Before You Arrive

What to Expect

Whether this is your first time in a church or you are returning after a long break — you are in the right place. Here is what a typical Sunday looks like at Bethel Baptist Church.

🤝

Warm Welcome

Our welcome team will greet you at the door, help you find a seat and answer any questions you have.

🎵

Worship

We open with 20–25 minutes of contemporary worship music — a mix of modern and traditional hymns.

📖

Message

A practical, Bible-based sermon of about 35–40 minutes. Relevant, engaging and always grounded in Scripture.

☕

Fellowship

After service we gather for refreshments. A great opportunity to meet people and feel at home.

👔

Dress Code

Smart casual — come as you are. We want you to feel comfortable. No strict dress code applies.

🅿️

Parking

Free parking is available on the church grounds. Additional street parking is available nearby.

👶

Children

We have a safe, fun children's programme running during every service for ages 0–12. Your children are always welcome.

⏱

Duration

Each service runs for approximately 90 minutes. We recommend arriving 10 minutes early.
