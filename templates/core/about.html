Who We Are

A community Built on Grace

Bethel Baptist Church was founded in ..... by ............. with a simple conviction that every person, regardless of background or story, deserves to experience the love of God and the warmth of genuine community.

What started as a gathering of small families in a rented hall in Accra has grown into a vibrant congregation of over 400 active members across multiple services, ministries, and outreach programmes.

We are not a perfect church — we are a church of people on a journey. Our doors are open to the curious, the doubting, the hurting, and the joyful alike.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These are not just words on a wall — they shape every decision, ever service, and every interaction in our community.

✝

Faith-Centres

Everything we do is rooted in the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Scripture guides our worship, our teaching, and our community life.

♥

Radically Welcoming

We believe the church should look like heaven — diverse, warm, and free of judgment. Everyone has a seat at the table.

◎

Community-Driven

We do life together. Through small groups, outreach, and shared meals, we build real relationships that go beyond Sunday.

◆

Spirit-Led

We create space for the Holy Spirit to move in our worship, our prayer, and our personal lives — expecting God to show up.

❖

Serving Others

Faith without works is incomplete. We are deeply committed to serving Accra and beyond through tangible acts of love.

◉

Growing Together

We champion lifelong discipleship — equipping every member to grow spiritually, practically, and in their calling.

What We Believe

Our Statement of Faith

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God — our complete guide for faith and life.

02

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

The Church

We believe the local church is God's primary instrument for transformation in the world.

04

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit indwells every believer and equips us with gifts for ministry and life.

05

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is a free gift received through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works.

06

The Return of Christ

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ and the resurrection of the dead.
